How to Record a Valid Unboxing Video:

  • Start before opening: Record all 4 sides of the outer shipping box or envelope showing it is completely sealed and untampered.
  • Shipping label clear: Point the camera at the shipping label so your Name and Order ID are clearly readable.
  • Continuous recording: Cut open the tape and unbox the products in one uninterrupted video (no pauses, cuts, or edits).
